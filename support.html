Frequently asked questions

Your workspace, explained.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No. BTSEWORKS has no account or sign-in and is designed to work offline. The app does not make network requests, so you can create and review records without an internet connection.

Where are my records and photos stored?

They are stored in BTSEWORKS's private application-support folder on your device. The app does not upload them to a developer-operated server or provide its own cloud sync. Device backups, if enabled, are managed separately by iOS, Apple or your computer.

How do I add a photo to a record?

While creating or editing a supported line, work order, shift log or defect record, choose the camera or photo-library option. Camera access lets you take a new photo; photo-library access lets you choose an existing image. Attached photos are stored in the app's private folder.

If access was previously denied, open iOS Settings, find BTSEWORKS, and enable the relevant permission. You can use the rest of the app without granting photo permissions.

How do I export my data?
  1. Open the Workspace tab.
  2. Under Export, choose Work orders, Lines, Shift logs or Defects.
  3. Tap Prepare CSV.
  4. Tap Share file and choose a destination in the iOS share sheet.

Each export contains every row in the selected dataset. The app creates the file locally and does not upload it. Exports contain the real figures even when the privacy mask is on, so check the file before sharing.

Why does the app contain sample records?

BTSEWORKS opens with an illustrative plant so you can explore its charts and workflows. Replace the sample entries with your own records when you are ready.

Important: “Restore sample workspace” replaces the entire current workspace with sample data. Export anything important before using that option.

How do I delete some or all of my data?

You can delete an individual record from its own screen. To clear the workspace, go to Workspace → Manage data → Delete all records. This removes all lines, work orders, shift logs, defect records and attached photos from the app, and cannot be undone.

Deleting BTSEWORKS removes its app-container data from the device. Previously exported CSV files are separate copies and must be deleted from wherever you saved or shared them.

What does the privacy mask hide?

The privacy mask in Workspace replaces displayed monetary amounts with dots. It is a display setting, not encryption, and it does not change your records. CSV exports still contain the actual figures.

How are OEE and cost figures calculated?

BTSEWORKS calculates its charts and figures from the records and plant settings you enter. OEE combines availability, performance and quality; cost views use your material, labour, overhead and scrap inputs. Check the underlying entries before relying on a result for scheduling, costing or other operational decisions.
