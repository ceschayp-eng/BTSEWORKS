Privacy Policy

This policy explains how BTSEWORKS handles the records you create, the permissions you choose to grant, and this support website.

BTSEWORKS is offline by design.

The app has no account system, makes no network requests, and does not send your production records or photos to the developer. There are no analytics, advertising, tracking or in-app purchases.

1. At a glance

  • Your work orders, production lines, shift logs, defect records, settings and attached photos are stored locally on your device.
  • We do not operate a server that receives your app data.
  • Camera and photo library access is used only when you choose to attach an image to a record.
  • CSV files are created only when you request an export. You decide whether and where to share them.
  • You can delete records and photos within the app or remove the app's local data by deleting the app.

2. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the BTSEWORKS iOS application (the “App”) and the static BTSEWORKS website hosted on GitHub Pages (the “Website”). The App and Website are different: the App does not make network requests, while visiting the Website necessarily involves your browser connecting to the website host.

3. Data stored on your device

The App stores the information you enter so that it can provide its production-record and reporting features. This may include:

  • Production line names, locations, statuses, operating targets and related notes.
  • Work order numbers, products, customers, quantities, dates, costs and other details you enter.
  • Shift dates, operator names, output, scrap, downtime, reasons and notes.
  • Defect categories, severity, quantities, root causes, dispositions and notes.
  • Plant settings, display preferences and photos you attach to records.

This information is saved in the App's private application-support folder in the iOS app sandbox. BTSEWORKS does not upload this information to us or make it available to other apps through a BTSEWORKS service.

4. Information the App does not collect

The App does not collect or transmit personal information, usage analytics, diagnostics, advertising identifiers or location data to the developer. It does not use third-party analytics, advertising or tracking services, and it does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

There is no BTSEWORKS account, sign-in, advertising profile, push-notification registration or developer-operated cloud database.

5. Camera and photo library

BTSEWORKS requests camera or photo library access only when you choose to add an image to a production line, work order, shift log or defect record.

  • Camera: used to take a photo you intentionally attach to a record.
  • Photo library: used to read an image you select for attachment.

Attached images may be resized and saved as JPEG files in the App's private folder. The App does not upload these images or use them for facial recognition, advertising or analytics. Denying access does not prevent you from using the rest of the App; you can continue without photo attachments. You can change permissions at any time in iOS Settings.

6. Exports and sharing

When you select a dataset and tap “Prepare CSV” in the Workspace tab, the App creates a CSV file on your device. Nothing is sent to the developer as part of this process.

If you use the iOS share sheet, the file is handled by the destination app or service you select, such as email, messaging, cloud storage or a computer. That destination's own privacy terms apply. Review exported files before sharing because they contain the figures and production information you entered. CSV exports contain the real figures even when the App's privacy mask is enabled.

7. Retention and deletion

Local records remain available until you change or delete them, clear the workspace, or delete the App. You can:

  • Delete an individual record from its own screen.
  • Go to Workspace → Manage data → Delete all records to remove all lines, work orders, shift logs, defects and attached photos.
  • Delete BTSEWORKS from your device to remove the App and its app-container data.

These actions cannot be undone within the App. Exported files are separate copies and are not deleted when you clear the App; delete those copies through the app or location where you stored them. Restoring the sample workspace replaces the current records with illustrative sample data.

8. Device backups

BTSEWORKS does not provide cloud sync or its own backup service. Depending on your iOS and backup settings, the operating system may include app data in a device backup managed by Apple or by your computer. We do not control those backups. Their storage, retention and deletion are governed by your device settings and the applicable backup provider's terms.

9. Security

App records and attached photos are stored in the App's iOS sandbox. You are responsible for protecting access to your device and for choosing secure destinations for exported files. No method of storage or device security is completely risk-free, so keep your device updated, use an appropriate device passcode and maintain your own backups of important exports.

10. Children's privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because app records stay on the user's device and are not transmitted to us, we do not receive or maintain children's personal information through the App.

11. This website

The Website contains static pages. We do not add analytics scripts, advertising trackers, cookies, account forms or databases to it. If you tap an email link, your chosen email app handles the message and any information you decide to send.

The Website is hosted using GitHub Pages. Like most hosting providers, GitHub may process technical information such as your IP address, browser information and request logs to deliver and secure the Website. We do not receive your BTSEWORKS app records through GitHub Pages. For details, see the GitHub General Privacy Statement and GitHub Pages documentation.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, Website or applicable requirements change. The revised version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Material changes may also be communicated through the App or its App Store listing where appropriate.

13. Contact
